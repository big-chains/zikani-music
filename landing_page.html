(ABOUT THE ARTIST)

Zikani is a visionary artist from Blantyre, Malawi, blending traditional Gule Wamkulu rhythms with cutting-edge Afro-beats and futuristic electronic soundscapes. Breaking boundaries and redefining the African sound, Zikani's music is a journey into the future of global music.
